A homemaker’s diary from the New Zealand countryside

More about the Delfinado’s

Hi, I’m Geraldine, a wife, a mum and a homemaker raising our family in the New Zealand countryside. Our days are small and ordinary: tending the garden, cooking from scratch, keeping the house, stretching the budget and celebrating birthdays around the kitchen table.

I film these quiet, everyday moments because they pass so quickly. Documenting them reminds me what truly matters, and I hope they encourage you to slow down and cherish your own home and family too.

I believe homemaking is a ministry. Our vision is to see more families thrive by choosing faith over fear and by putting family first.
